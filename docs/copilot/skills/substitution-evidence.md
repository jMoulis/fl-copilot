# Product Copilot skill contract — Daily substitution evidence

Status: M6-T06 deterministic remote computation and offline read-only views implemented. Conversational registration remains M7; model scoring is M6-T07/T08. This is an application skill contract, not a Codex development skill.

Read current scoped evidence and its processing state, exact source product/candidate/customer need/event, original interval, daily observation/reference values and lineage/context snapshots. Explain unavailable days, unusable reference, overlapping duplicates, planned vs declared tasks and context gaps. Queued/error data may be an older retained comparison; do not present it as newly computed.

The shared buildDailySubstitutionEvidence operation and versioned rule calculate comparisons from trusted canonical published observations. Models cannot supply official metrics, source IDs, quality weights or causal conclusions. Same-weekday references are explicit and incomparable/incident/operation days are excluded; a source monetary increase is a qualified co-movement, not a unit-volume response or an effect caused by a stockout. Missing is not zero, forecasts are not observed weather, and planned operations are not full execution.

The authenticated executor owns store scope, canonical input snapshots, leases and publication. Source mutations enqueue work transactionally; a signed remote recovery computes/revises evidence, and the client receives read-only records through normal sync. Source corrections retain immutable historical snapshots. AI may summarize/explain the results and ask meaningful questions; it must not edit evidence or silently validate/reactivate a relation. Do not demand completion of every product record merely to discuss an available comparison.

Report facts, interpretation and recommended action separately. State daily granularity and uncertainty plainly. Relationship learning/confidence and accepted recommendation/executed action remain separate audited operations with their own explicit safeguards.
