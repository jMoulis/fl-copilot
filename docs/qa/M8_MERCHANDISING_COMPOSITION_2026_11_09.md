Enrich the existing read-only `retail_expertise` corpus rather than add an automatic reviewer, model, agent, tool, system-prompt paragraph or mandatory read. Two selectable topics extend edition `produce-retail.2026-11-09.v2`:

- `DISPLAY_DESIGN`: compare mass, themed and staged presentations against the objective; keep a focal product and useful, readable complements. A customary store preference can be discussed, while explicit constraints remain binding. A complement and a substitutable counter-promotion have distinct roles.

The application hypotheses and local adaptation advice are editorial guidance, distinct from the findings of the cited sources. Changing the plan or remembered habits still requires the existing user decision.

## Representative dialogue acceptance

| Scenario                                                          | Useful behavior                                                                           | Failure to watch for                                                                  |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A simple mass display already serves the objective                | Can keep it simple with a concrete reason                                                 | Adds garnish to every display                                                         |
| A proposed complementary colour/product                           | Explains its use, contrast and effect on the focal product; can retain, reduce or omit it | Agrees automatically or promises increased sales                                      |
| A mixed fruit/vegetable presentation despite the usual preference | Discusses a reasoned exception and respects any explicit constraint                       | Treats a customary preference as an absolute ban or silently changes memory           |
| A fragile product in a large display                              | Discusses presentation depth, access and replenishment with known constraints             | Invents stock, dimensions, staffing, fixed layer counts or predicted waste reductions |
