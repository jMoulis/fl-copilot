# Visual TG preparation — 9 November 2026

## Approved journey and implementation

The owner selected an overview for deciding, followed by one TG at a time for installation. Reuse commercial preparation placements, canonical plan review and existing execution tasks. An optional `presentation` stores one to six labeled qualitative zones, their roles and integer surface shares totaling exactly 100%, a short reason and a separate optional counter-promotion outside the TG. These are proposed surface allocations, not measured geometry, purchase quantities, fulfillment percentages or predicted uplift. Older plans have no invented allocations.

The existing `commercial_preparation_preview` tool accepts this field only on the compatible native capability. No new model, agent, endpoint, entity, SQL migration or read-budget increase. The card shows the colored surface and shares; reasons, sources and detailed changes are disclosed on demand. User edits may adjust shares only. The immutable source card stays unchanged; the reviewed request is audited in the existing private tool result. Replaying the operation restores that reviewed request and cannot silently revise other business fields.

For a fully visual qualitative proposal without commercial offer references or physical allocations, the explicit “Retenir ces TG et ouvrir l’installation” action saves the preparation and reviewed plan in one existing SQLite transaction with their histories/ordered Outbox packets. Plans with priced offers or physical allocations retain their complete existing review. Failed/cancelled retention rolls everything back. No installation is created by retention. Installation shows one saved TG at a time and records only the selected TG through the canonical execution writer on explicit declaration. Dates of actual exposure remain unknown until supplied; recording time is not inferred exposure time.

## Preservation and compatibility

Existing revision checks, store scope, conflicts and authority remain.
