# M7 — original PDF offer selection (implementation in progress)

## Intended complete flow

Read the real PDF in the conversation, discuss any requested week, identify the intended product and terms, show the proposed offer with its original page/excerpt, then obtain the manager's explicit decision. Save through the existing atomic local choice writer, Outbox and authenticated sync. Reuse canonical validation, preparation and plan services. No full-page AI extraction or review campaign may be a prerequisite for this path.

The original/excerpt is not a fabricated `commercialVisualReadings` record. Existing extracted source coordinates retain their exact identity, behavior and history. An original reference identifies the confirmed source checksum, actual PDF page and displayed excerpt; it does not claim automatic visual/text verification or supply fictional confidence/response metadata. The model cannot set approvals, actor authority or durable source checksums. Human review and native confirmation remain mandatory for business persistence.
