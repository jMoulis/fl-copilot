# Store photographs in the Copilot — V1

M7/L7 extension of the conversational delivery plan. A department photograph is an immutable `STORE_PHOTO` source, not a waste receipt, product-library asset, commercial PDF, inventory count or physical-plan measurement.

The native gallery flow retains an app-owned original offline. Source metadata, file, existing upload job and private conversation draft attachment commit together. Capture/reattachment is explicit, bounded and scope checked. Failed/cancelled transactions never remove selected originals or uncertain private copies. Source upload resumes through the existing queue. Its durable job binds the capturing owner; account switches cannot claim, display or upload another owner’s photographs, and credentials are checked again when initiating/completing the upload. Photograph upload never dispatches receipt vision or commercial-PDF processing.

The existing conversational agent sees a verified, oriented JPEG through the AI SDK's multimodal tool output. No second extraction model, automatic AI call on upload, new provider or business mutation is implied. The authenticated read is limited to confirmed photographs uploaded by the same owner in the authorized store and referenced in the private generation context. It verifies original bytes, checksum, MIME, dimensions and the retained upload binding; it rechecks availability after reading. Images, private paths, storage URLs and tokens never become text facts, logs or stored conversation messages.

Source limits: JPEG/PNG/HEIC/HEIF, 20 MiB and 36 million pixels. The model frame is at most 2048 pixels on either edge, 2 MiB, stripped of EXIF. Per-turn image count and the existing read budget are enforced without silently omitting photos. The original remains unchanged. Native source viewing and upload retry preserve the distinction between local retained file, pending upload and model access.

The source evidence attests to availability of that photograph, not the truth of an inferred product, stock, price, dimension, business date or causal claim. Qualitative visual findings remain interpretations, with visible limitations. Official figures still come from canonical tools; uncertain numeric labels/units/identities require the established confirmation/form path. The agent may discuss presentation, signage, visible product families and merchandising hypotheses using the actual image and existing expert/context tools. It does not identify or assess people. An image is neither a validated store-plan reference nor permission to execute a business action.

Acceptance: offline capture/restart and atomic rollback; source upload without receipt/PDF jobs; same-owner/store/private-context isolation; bounded genuine images and tampered/missing bytes; EXIF/orientation handling; explicit original/source viewing; actual image content reaching a simulated existing agent; unavailable/missing/retired sources fail closed; existing numeric grounding and action authority remain unchanged. Real model quality, camera/Photos permissions, offline/reconnect and accessibility require the grouped native pilot. No paid test or EAS is part of implementation verification.
